## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Equipment Source Model
The system SHALL represent equipment affix pools by canonical TLIDB source names and SHALL support the requested armor, weapon, shield, and accessory equipment families.

#### Scenario: Supported equipment sources
- **WHEN** equipment affix definitions are loaded
- **THEN** the system SHALL expose source pools for strength, dexterity, and wisdom head/chest/gloves/boots, strength/dexterity/wisdom shields, TLIDB weapon sources, necklace, ring, belt, and spirit ring sources.

#### Scenario: Source pool isolation
- **WHEN** an equipment item is generated for `力量头部`
- **THEN** generated affixes SHALL come only from `力量头部` pools and SHALL NOT use `敏捷头部`, weapon, or accessory affix rows.

### Requirement: Fixed Base Affix
Every equipment item SHALL have exactly one base affix rolled from its source's `基础词缀` pool, and base affix tiers SHALL NOT be gated by equipment level.

#### Scenario: Low-level base affix can use any base tier
- **WHEN** a level 1 equipment item is generated
- **THEN** the base affix candidate pool SHALL include all matching source base affixes regardless of the base affix row's TLIDB tier.

#### Scenario: Base affix is always present
- **WHEN** a white, blue, purple, or pink equipment item is generated
- **THEN** the generated item SHALL contain exactly one base affix.

### Requirement: Rarity Ordinary Affix Counts
Equipment rarity SHALL determine the target number of ordinary non-base affixes before level capacity is applied.

#### Scenario: Rarity targets
- **WHEN** equipment is generated by rarity
- **THEN** white SHALL target 0 ordinary affixes, blue SHALL target 1-2, purple SHALL target 3-5, and pink SHALL target 6.

#### Scenario: Level capacity caps rarity target
- **WHEN** a level 76 pink equipment item is generated
- **THEN** the system SHALL cap ordinary affixes at the level 76 maximum of 3 prefixes and 2 suffixes.

### Requirement: Level Prefix Suffix Capacity
Equipment level SHALL define the maximum prefix and suffix slots available on an item.

#### Scenario: Capacity table
- **WHEN** equipment level is evaluated
- **THEN** levels 1-10 SHALL allow at most 1 prefix and 0 suffixes, 11-25 at most 1 prefix and 1 suffix, 26-40 at most 2 prefixes and 1 suffix, 41-60 at most 2 prefixes and 2 suffixes, 61-80 at most 3 prefixes and 2 suffixes, and 81+ at most 3 prefixes and 3 suffixes.

### Requirement: Generated Initial And Advanced Tiers
The system SHALL generate missing T2-T7 rows for initial and advanced affixes from each TLIDB T1 row using the agreed level, weight, and value scaling table.

#### Scenario: Tier unlock table
- **WHEN** initial or advanced affix candidates are generated
- **THEN** T1 SHALL require level 86 and weight 100, T2 level 82 and weight 200, T3 level 76 and weight 800, T4 level 68 and weight 3200, T5 level 58 and weight 3200, T6 level 40 and weight 6250, and T7 level 1 and weight 6250.

#### Scenario: T1 remains source value
- **WHEN** a T1 generated definition is read
- **THEN** its effect text SHALL preserve the TLIDB T1 row values.

#### Scenario: T3 value is scaled from T1
- **WHEN** `+(19-27)% 火焰抗性` is generated as T3
- **THEN** the T3 effect text SHALL use approximately 70% of the T1 numeric range.

### Requirement: Ordinary Affix Pool Selection
The system SHALL choose an affix library before rolling a concrete prefix or suffix affix.

#### Scenario: Crafting chooses library first
- **WHEN** crafting requests an initial suffix on a level 76 `力量头部`
- **THEN** candidates SHALL be selected from initial suffixes for `力量头部` and eligible tiers T3-T7 only.

#### Scenario: Random generation chooses ordinary libraries
- **WHEN** random equipment generation fills ordinary affix slots
- **THEN** it SHALL choose between initial and advanced libraries before selecting a concrete affix, and SHALL NOT randomly generate pinnacle affixes.

### Requirement: Advanced And Pinnacle Caps
Each equipment item SHALL have at most 2 advanced affixes and at most 2 pinnacle affixes.

#### Scenario: Advanced cap
- **WHEN** an item already has 2 advanced affixes
- **THEN** generation or crafting SHALL NOT add another advanced affix.

#### Scenario: Pinnacle level gate
- **WHEN** crafting requests a pinnacle affix on equipment below level 100
- **THEN** the system SHALL reject the craft.

#### Scenario: Pinnacle cap
- **WHEN** an item already has 2 pinnacle affixes
- **THEN** crafting SHALL NOT add another pinnacle affix.

### Requirement: Probability Candidate Pools
The generator SHALL make probability-sensitive candidate pools inspectable so expected probabilities can be validated.

#### Scenario: Level 76 strength head fire resistance T3
- **WHEN** the level 76 `力量头部` initial suffix candidate pool is inspected
- **THEN** it SHALL include `火焰抗性` T3 with weight 800 and SHALL include only T3-T7 tiers for all initial suffix families.
